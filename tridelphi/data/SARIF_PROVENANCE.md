**Note for `sarif-contract`:** this schema declares
`"$schema": "http://json-schema.org/draft-04/schema#"`. Select the validator
from that key (`jsonschema.validators.validator_for`) rather than assuming a
modern draft — Draft7/2020-12 validators silently mis-handle draft-04 keywords
such as `exclusiveMinimum` and boolean `required`.
